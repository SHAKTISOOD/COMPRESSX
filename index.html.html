Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress a PDF?

Upload your PDF, choose the desired reduction percentage, and click Compress Files.

How do I compress a JPG or JPEG?

Upload your JPG or JPEG image, choose a compression percentage, and click Compress Files.

Can I compress PNG files?

Yes. PNG files are supported by CompressX. Image compression creates an optimized JPEG output.

Can I choose the compression amount?

Yes. The compression control lets you choose a reduction target from 10% to 90%.

Is CompressX free?
